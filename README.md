# 🛍️ Vrinda Store Annual Report — Excel Dashboard

An end-to-end data analysis project on Vrinda Store's e-commerce sales data, built entirely in **Microsoft Excel**.  
The goal was to analyze 12 months of sales data and generate actionable business insights through an interactive dashboard.

## 🎯 Objective

To help Vrinda Store understand:
- Which sales channels are performing best
- Who their primary customers are (age, gender)
- Which states generate the most revenue
- How efficiently orders are being fulfilled

## 📈 Key Insights

### 🛒 Sales Channels
- **Amazon** is the top-performing channel at **35.48%** of total sales
- **Myntra** (23.36%) and **Flipkart** (21.59%) are strong secondary channels
- Ajio contributes only 6.22% — potential area for growth

### 👩 Customer Demographics
- **Women account for 64%** of total purchases vs 36% Men
- **Adult women (54.15%)** are the single largest buyer segment
- Senior and Teenager segments are relatively untapped

### 📍 Top Revenue States
| State | Revenue |
|---|---|
| Maharashtra | 3.0M |
| Karnataka | 2.6M |
| Uttar Pradesh | 2.1M |
| Tamil Nadu | 1.7M |
| Telangana | 1.7M |

### 📦 Order Status
- **92% of orders** were successfully delivered
- Returns: 3% | Cancellations: 3% | Refunds: 2%
- Very strong fulfillment performance overall

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. **Double down on Amazon & Myntra** — highest ROI channels
2. **Target adult women** in marketing campaigns — dominant buyer segment
3. **Focus ad spend on Maharashtra & Karnataka** — highest revenue states
4. **Investigate Ajio performance** — low share despite being a major platform
5. **Reduce return & cancellation rate** further by improving product descriptions

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download the `Vrinda_Store_Analysis.xlsx` file
2. Open in Microsoft Excel (2016 or later recommended)
3. Use the **Slicers** on the left panel to filter by:
   - 📅 Month
   - 🏷️ Category
   - 🛒 Channel
4. Dashboard updates dynamically based on your selection
